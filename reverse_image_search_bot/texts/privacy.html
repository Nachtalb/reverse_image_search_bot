🔒 Privacy This explains what the bot stores about you, who else sees it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. Contact report@nachtalb.io What we store • Your Telegram profile: user id, username, first and last name, language code, when we first and last saw you. • Uploads: every image, sticker or video you send for a search. For a video we store one still frame. We also keep the upload record: Telegram file ids, file type, time, caption, and the group or channel it came through. • Chats: id, title and username of groups and channels the bot is used in, plus the settings chosen with /settings. • Feedback: messages sent via /feedback, and replies to them. • Usage metrics: aggregate counters (searches per engine, commands used). Not tied to your data. Who else sees it Reverse image search only works by handing your image to search engines. Each upload is stored on our server under a public, unguessable link and that link is sent to the engines you pick — SauceNAO, Google, Yandex, Bing, TinEye, IQDB, ascii2d, Sogou, AnimeTrace, Trace.moe and similar. What those services do with the image is governed by their own policies. Anyone with the link can open the image while it exists. Operators of the bot may review uploads to handle abuse and TOS violations. How long • Uploaded files are deleted from our server after 30 days. • Upload records and profile data are kept until you delete them. • Chat settings are kept until you delete them. • Data connected to an abuse report is kept for as long as required for legal purposes. It is stored separately with restricted access, and it is not part of /takeout or /delete. Your options • /takeout — get a zip with a JSON of the records we hold and the uploaded files still on our server. • /delete — remove your profile, settings, upload records and files from our server. In a group these commands act on the group's data and can only be run by its admins. Both ask for confirmation first. Data we are required to keep for legal purposes is not affected by /delete.
